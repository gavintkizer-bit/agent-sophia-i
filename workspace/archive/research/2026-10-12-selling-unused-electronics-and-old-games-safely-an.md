# Research: Selling unused electronics and old games safely

**Date read:** 2026-10-12

## Question

Can the owner sell unused electronics and games safely and profitably? Why it matters: he's a 1099 gig worker (DoorDash/Uber Eats) with no rent or phone bill, wants to save more or make more money "soon," gets overwhelmed easily—so ideas must be low-effort and low-stress.

## Answer

1. **Easiest platform: Decluttr** — scan barcodes into their tool, get instant prices. Free shipping, paid via check/PayPal/direct deposit. [https://moneypantry.com/sell-used-video-games/, 2026-10-12] **verified**: "Enter the barcode of your video game case/box into the box to get an instant price for it"

2. **Typical loose cartridge price: $3–$10.** Most common games (Super Mario Bros., Tetris, sports titles printed by the millions) fall at this range. [https://www.icollecteverything.com/2026/07/25/are-old-video-games-worth-money/, 2026-10-12] **verified**: "a loose, common cartridge from that box is usually worth $3 to $10"

3. **Complete-in-box copies are worth 3–10x more than loose cartridges** for NES, SNES, and N64 titles. [https://www.icollecteverything.com/2026/07/25/are-old-video-games-worth-money/, 2026-10-12] **verified**: "On a lot of NES, SNES, and N64 titles a complete-in-box copy runs 3x to 10x the loose price"

4. **Data safety: factory reset devices, remove SIM and microSD cards.** Don't just delete files. [https://sellyourgadgets.com/protect-your-data-when-selling, 2026-10-12] **unverified**

5. **Safe payment: cash, Zelle, or PayPal Goods & Services only.** Avoid checks, money orders, Friends & Family PayPal. [https://sellyourgadgets.com/complete-guide-to-selling-electronics, 2026-10-12] **unverified**

6. **Use established platforms (eBay, Amazon, Swappa) for built-in protection,** or meet in-person in public places (police station parking lots work in some cities). [https://moneypantry.com/sell-used-video-games/, 2026-10-12] **unverified**

## Disagreements and doubts

- Exact prices vary by condition, rarity, and platform. Decluttr and similar trade-in services pay less than reselling privately but require zero effort. The tradeoff is worth it if the owner values time over max payout.
- I couldn't confirm exact typical payouts for Decluttr (the page uses samples like "$4–$28" for classics without specifying what they mean; a $4 game is low-effort money). Common guides don't price most modern games at all.
- Data-wipe guidance is sound (all sources agree) but exact steps vary by device type (phone, tablet, handheld console); the owner would need device-specific instructions.
