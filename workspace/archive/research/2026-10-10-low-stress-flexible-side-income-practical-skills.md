# Research: Low-stress, flexible side income using practical skills (cooking, cleaning, lawn care, handyman work, box truck driving)

**Date:** 2026-10-10  
**For:** a young adult who works as a food delivery driver (DoorDash/Uber Eats, 5-star), earns 1099 income, may move to a ski town (Breckenridge, Colorado) for a winter job with 2 days off per week, gets overwhelmed easily, so ideas must be low-effort and low-stress.

## Question
What low-stress, practical side income options exist for a delivery driver with skills in cooking, cleaning, lawn care, handyman work, and box truck driving, that can work around a seasonal job in a ski town?

## Answer

**Practical side income options:**

1. **Cleaning services** (residential move-outs, home cleaning): $33–$100/hour effective rate ($400–$700 per move-out job, typically 2–3 hours work). Low decision burden: customers set scope, pay rates are transparent on platforms like Handy. [https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Flexible-Side, read 2026-10-10] **unverified**

2. **Handyman services** (hanging curtains, painting, small repairs): $50–$85 per hour. Platforms like Handy and Thumbtack connect you with local clients; seasonal ski towns have high turnover and demand for this work. [https://www.ziprecruiter.com/Jobs/Flexible-Side, read 2026-10-10] **unverified**

3. **Box truck/cargo services** (GoShare, last-mile delivery, moving assistance): Drivers with larger vehicles earn more than standard delivery. Works well in ski towns with seasonal relocations and tourism. [https://www.blog.jointaggr.com/best-side-hustles-for-delivery-drivers, read 2026-10-10] **unverified**

4. **Courier work** (medical deliveries, retail drops, business runs): Services like Roadie offer "more structure than food apps" with scheduled blocks rather than individual orders, reducing decision fatigue. Independent of meal-time demand. [https://www.blog.jointaggr.com/best-side-hustles-for-delivery-drivers, read 2026-10-10] **unverified**

5. **Package delivery with schedule control**: Amazon Flex and similar services provide scheduled delivery blocks you can choose, avoiding the real-time scramble of food apps. [https://www.blog.jointaggr.com/best-side-hustles-for-delivery-drivers, read 2026-10-10] **unverified**

6. **Expense tracking is critical**: Apps like Fuelly or Simply Auto help you stay profitable. The IRS currently allows $0.67 per mile deduction for professional drivers. Track this to avoid tax surprises on 1099 income. [https://www.thedrive.com/news/44922/how-to-avoid-scams-as-a-rideshare-and-delivery-driver, read 2026-10-10] **unverified**

**Red flags to avoid:**
- "No legitimate gig will make you pay for expenses, training, or anything else to get the job." Any upfront fee = scam. [https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2026/02/how-avoid-side-hustle-scam, read 2026-10-10] **unverified** (source returned 404 on verify attempt, but FTC.gov is primary/official on scam warnings; fact is reliable even unverified here)
- Beware ads promising "fast cash" for "minimal work" or extreme time pressure ("act now or it disappears"). Real employers give time to decide. [https://www.thedrive.com/news/44922/how-to-avoid-scams-as-a-rideshare-and-delivery-driver, read 2026-10-10] **unverified**

**Selection strategy for overwhelm avoidance:**
When comparing options, rank each by: (a) pay during your actual available hours, (b) startup cost, (c) distance between tasks, (d) how much schedule control you have, (e) how reliable the work is. Don't chase advertised rates; focus on what you'll *actually* earn during 2 days off per week. [https://www.blog.jointaggr.com/best-side-hustles-for-delivery-drivers, read 2026-10-10] **unverified**

## Disagreements and Doubts
- **Hourly rate claims vary widely** ($33–$100/hr for cleaning alone) depending on location, season, and demand. A ski town in winter may see much higher rates than the national averages quoted. No source addresses Breckenridge specifically.
- **Income volatility**: no source addresses how seasonal ski-town work (winter job) affects side income availability Dec–Apr. If his main job is busy those months, side work may be limited then.
- **Tax complexity on multiple 1099s**: all sources mention tracking expenses, but none address the complexity of filing multiple 1099-NEC or 1099-K forms (DoorDash, Uber Eats, plus new side gig). He may need a tax professional.
