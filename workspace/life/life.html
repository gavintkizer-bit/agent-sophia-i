Life Lab

Conway's Game of Life, built as a lab. Every cell follows the same rule, so any run can be repeated exactly from its seed. Draw on the grid, or fill it at random and press Play.

World rules

Conway's rule: a dead cell with 3 neighbors is born; a live cell with 2 or 3 survives.
