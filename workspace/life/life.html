Life Lab

Simple rules, surprising worlds. Play with a world on the Board, or test an idea in Experiment or Numbers.

1. Pick a world

Conway's rule: a dead cell with 3 neighbors is born; a live cell with 2 or 3 survives.

2. Set the start

Draw on the grid with your finger or mouse, or fill it at random. The same density and seed always make the same start.

3. Keep a find

Saves the board exactly as it is now (cells, world, rule, size, edges) so you can load it later.

Experiment

Which starting density does what? The lab fills many worlds at random, runs each one without drawing, and counts. It uses the Life rule, size and edges from the Board (B3/S23, 96 x 60, wrap).

Heads up: this isn't Conway's Life. The Board's rule is (the Fractal seed button sets it). Experiments will use that rule.

Numbers

Do "special" numbers make special worlds? Each number becomes a starting world. Your numbers are ranked against every number in the range: a special one should land near the top, an ordinary one near the middle.

Write the claim and your prediction to unlock Run.

Self-test

Checks the engine against patterns whose behavior is known, before we trust any experiment. Every check has one right answer.
