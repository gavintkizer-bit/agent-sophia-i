# Colorado: the soft move (planning notes, 2026-10-09)

Owner, the night before the Black Tie Breck interview: "I have been passing up on this opportunity every year for 5
years now and I am finally ready for it." Furnished place in Breckenridge, $1,400 a month with all utilities; the
landlord is relaxed and would likely take half a month's rent for a soft start. These are his decisions; I plan
and check, he chooses. Ties to his goals: steady job, move to Colorado, Quandary Peak, saving.

## The car question (fly first, or drive?)
- **Lean: drive, if the tires pass and the move is before mid-November.** It's about 1,650 miles (about 3 days).
  The car carries far more, and a second trip back for it costs a flight plus 3 more driving days.
- **Must-check before driving:** tire tread (a quarter in the groove; if you can see the top of Washington's head,
  the tread is getting low), and whether the tires say M+S. Colorado's traction law on the I-70 mountain stretch
  needs good-tread M+S or winter tires, chains, or a textile "snow sock" when it's in effect. Snow socks are
  cheaper than winter tires and fold into the trunk. [To verify on codot.gov before he goes.]
- **Fly first if:** they need you for training within days, the tires are worn, or a big storm is forecast.

## Soft-move pack list (furnished place, so: you, not furniture)
- Documents: ID and driver's license, Social Security card or passport (new-hire paperwork), car title and
  insurance card, bank card, a copy of the lease.
- Mountain clothes: warm layers (base layer, fleece, waterproof shell), winter boots, gloves, hat, wool socks.
  It's 9,600 ft: sunglasses, sunscreen, lip balm, a refillable water bottle.
- Work: phone, charger and car mount, a backup battery, comfortable work shoes.
- Car kit: ice scraper and snow brush, blanket, jumper cables, phone charger, water and snacks, small shovel.
- Personal: meds and a copy of prescriptions, toiletries, laptop and charger. Bring what you love, leave the rest.

## Contingencies (if X happens, then Y)
1. **The job doesn't come through:** Breckenridge hires a lot of seasonal staff in fall; apply to 2-3 others
   before you leave (rental shops, resorts, delivery). You can also stay in Charlotte rent-free and try again.
2. **Money runs short:** keep a cushion of 2 months' rent plus gas home before you go (money ideas #1, #4, #5).
   Gig delivery apps may work in Summit County as a fallback.
3. **The season ends (spring):** Black Tie is seasonal. Decide by February: stay for summer work, or come home.
4. **Altitude:** first days, drink lots of water, go easy, no hard hikes yet (Quandary waits until you're used to it).
5. **Paperwork after you arrive:** tell Nationwide your new address (rates change by state), and look up when
   Colorado wants a new license and registration. [Exact deadlines to verify on colorado.gov.]
6. **Coming home:** your Charlotte home stays an option. A soft move is a test, not a promise.
