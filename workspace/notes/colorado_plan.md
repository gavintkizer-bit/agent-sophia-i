## The car (if driving)
- About 1,650 miles, about 3 days.
- **Tires:** quarter test (if you can see the top of Washington's head, the tread is low) and look for M+S.
- **Traction law (checked on codot.gov 2026-10-09, quotes verified):** when it's on, AWD/4WD cars may rely on good
  M+S/winter tires with 3/16" tread, but every other car needs "Chains or an approved alternative traction device".
  The Malibu is front-wheel drive: carry chains or approved snow socks (AutoSock is on CDOT's approved list). In late
  November the law is often on. "CDOT can implement traction and chain laws on any state highway". Alerts: COtrip.org.
