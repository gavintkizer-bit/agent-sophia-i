# Black Tie Breck: interview notes (owner's own notes, typed during the Zoom, 2026-10-09)

Tidied by Sophia; his words in quotes where unclear. Both unclear items confirmed with him the same day.

## Dates
- Training: Nov 30 through Dec 4 (2026).

## Pay
- New hires: $18.50/hour, plus a small bonus for working the whole season.
- Tips: credit-card tips go into a pool and are split; cash tips too. Tips average a consistent ~$9/hour
  (confirmed by him), so about $27.50, call it ~$28, an hour all in.
- Overtime: time and a half, during the three busiest weeks (confirmed by him).

## Perks
- Free Epic Pass.
- 65% off pro brands for skis.

## Schedule (full time)
- Two mornings and three nights a week, two days off.
- Morning call time: 6:45 am.

## Rough math (Sophia, before taxes, a full-time week of ~40 hours)
- About $27.50 x 40 = $1,100 a week, so the $1,400 monthly rent is roughly 1.3 weeks of pay.
