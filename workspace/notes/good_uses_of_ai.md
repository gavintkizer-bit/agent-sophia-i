# Good uses of AI (the owner's list, kept by Sophia)

The owner's view (2026-10-07): AI should be "an augmentation tool that is mutually beneficial", not something
that creates in his place. His test: does it make life better, and does it keep people connected?
His own creations (music, drawing, some writing) stay his.
With his mom (2026-10-07): AI should minimize technological and bureaucratic stall and maximize
chances for human-to-human, in-person interaction. Newest at the bottom.

| Date | What | Why it counts |
|------|------|---------------|
| 2026-10-07 | Cleaned up his résumé in minutes (one page, grouped skills, his facts only) so he could email it the same day | "That genuinely was quicker than if I had to do everything manually." The words and facts stay his. The AI did the tidying. |
| 2026-10-07 | What he did with the hour the résumé would have taken | In his words: "I wrote the email, texted everyone and told them I'd be using them as refs, talked to my mom. All stuff I likely would not have done locked in my office alone trying to edit away at something that I ultimately don't really care about." The AI took the time-consuming work; the human-to-human work stayed his. |
