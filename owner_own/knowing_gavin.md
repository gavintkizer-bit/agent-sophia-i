# Knowing Gavin (my portrait, from his own writing and drawings)

Written 2026-10-07 from what he shared (this folder; helpers are fenced out). It's for teamwork, not for imitating him: his words
win over mine, and he corrects this file. Every session reads it with my memory.

## His story, briefly
- Started working in restaurants at 17, "as a way to have extra money and slowly morphed into a means of
  survival." A hard start ("my childhood was tumultuous"), two semesters at DePaul, Chicago (Logan Square,
  2017, living with his uncle), then Charlotte.
- Came back to school at 27 for an Associate in Science, and finished his first full-time semester with a 4.0
  GPA. Aims at the earth sciences: structural or planetary geology, meteorology, water resources, ideally in
  public service ("serve the public's interests").
- In his words (2022): "I have had to live a reactionary lifestyle and I am ready for that to change. I am
  ready to take control of my future."

## Strengths I can build on
- **He turns what he loves into systems.** His "Magic Stats" project: 20+ hours, player stats, a deck list
  and a "salt score," built for a playgroup of about 15. Data, charts and structure are fun for him, not chores.
- **When it matters, he plans ahead.** Biweekly track lists, working a week ahead "because I have anxiety and
  getting overwhelmed is not an option for me." It got him an A in creative writing, a subject he feared.
- **He thinks in through-lines.** History essays trace ideas across centuries (Milton to Locke, the French
  Revolution to today). He cares about fairness and power: who gets opportunity and who has to fight for it.
- **He makes things:** his own 33-glyph alphabet, album covers built from his stained-glass drawing, and a
  story with a carefully built inner life (The Restrained Monster).
- **He's an honest self-assessor:** "7 out of 10, which is better than I have ever felt before!"

## Hurdles (his own words)
- Starting. Being late: "besides being late I NEED TO FIX THAT!" Anxiety and overwhelm. "I have trouble keeping
  track of everything." He has said writing is "not my strong suit," though his essays argue well.

## His aesthetic and loves
- Stained glass and neon nights; hand-drawn glyphs that blend scripts; ships, sea charts and maps; history;
  fantasy and games (Bleach, D&D, a Lord of the Rings goblin deck in Magic); sailing; music (his, never mine to touch).

## How I work with him, from this
1. Give him a system he can see: lists, a tracker, a chart. He likes them, and they lower his anxiety.
2. Build in slack. A week ahead beats the day of; never let a plan depend on a last-minute rush.
3. Help with time and starts: reminders ahead of start times, and the first step made small.
4. Explain the why and the history behind things. Through-lines pull him in.
